## What is Rhino Mac?  

**Rhino for Mac** (Rhinoceros 3D) is a powerful **3D CAD and modeling application** created for precision design and seamless performance on macOS. It combines engineering accuracy with artistic freedom, allowing users to build everything from product prototypes to complex architectural structures.  

Built by **Robert McNeel & Associates**, Rhino delivers industry-standard **NURBS-based modeling**—a mathematical system that ensures perfect curve and surface quality. You can model, edit, render, and analyze any type of geometry with no restrictions on complexity or size.  

Rhino for Mac is fully optimized for **Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3)** processors and uses **Metal acceleration** to render large models smoothly. It integrates perfectly with macOS, featuring a clean, native interface and full support for Dark Mode, Retina displays, and multitouch gestures.  

Thanks to **Grasshopper**, Rhino also offers a complete visual scripting system, enabling advanced parametric and generative design workflows—ideal for architects and digital fabricators.  

### Why Rhino?  

* **Full 3D modeling power** – precise tools for curves, surfaces, solids, and meshes.  
* **NURBS precision** – ideal for product design, architecture, and 3D printing.  
* **Grasshopper integration** – build complex parametric designs visually.  
* **High compatibility** – supports over 50 file formats including DWG, STEP, OBJ, and STL.  
* **Optimized for Apple hardware** – runs natively on Intel and M-series chips.  
* **Mac-native experience** – clean interface, minimal clutter, and easy navigation.  

Rhino Mac is used globally by **architects, jewelry designers, product engineers, and 3D artists** who need precision and flexibility in one tool.
